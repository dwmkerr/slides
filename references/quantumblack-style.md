This is an unofficial approximation based exclusively on publicly available visual material. It is not a QuantumBlack or McKinsey brand guide, product, or endorsement.

## Visual language

- Dark navy: `#051c2c`
- Electric blue: `#2251ff`
- White and cool grey copy
- Strong editorial typography
- Sparse, technical diagrams and precise alignment
