Put the idea to work

Make the next review easier

A small investment in evidence gathering makes every later change easier to inspect

Capture the result

  • Choose one visible behaviourMake the claim specific enough to demonstrate
  • Record the starting point
  • Capture the same workflow after the change
  • Keep the final frame visible long enough to read

Explain what changed

  • Put the evidence next to the claim
  • Link to a working previewLet someone try the interaction themselves
  • Include the relevant checks
  • Describe the limits of the example

Make it repeatable

  • Keep the capture script with the project
  • Use stable inputs for comparisons
  • Feed review findings back into the tooling
  • Share a short guide for the next contributor
